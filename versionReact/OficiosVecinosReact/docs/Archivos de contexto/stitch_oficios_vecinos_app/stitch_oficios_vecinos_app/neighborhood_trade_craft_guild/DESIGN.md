## Brand & Style

The design system is grounded in civic trust, neighborhood accessibility, and dependable craftsmanship. Designed to bridge everyday householders with vetted local trade workers (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, masonry, and painters), the aesthetic unites the utility of modern trade tools with the welcoming safety of a tight-knit community network.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate Tactility** with **Warm Civic Functionalism**:
- **Clarity and Dependability:** Uncluttered layouts, strong visual anchors, and uncompromised legibility for high-stress domestic repair moments.
- **Human Connection & Proximity:** Friendly spatial geometry, generous touch targets, and verified visual signifiers (badges, credentials, direct ratings).
- **Practical Utility:** Resilient visual affordances that read instantly in varying outdoor and domestic lighting environments, prioritizing instant comprehension over decorative complexity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate foundation of trust, civic security, and high-readability utilitarian contrast.

- **Primary (`#1764AE` - Royal Guild Blue):** The primary identity anchor. Used for principal interaction affordances, primary button fills, verified badges, active selection rings, and key navigation states.
- **Secondary (`#00112B` - Deep Midnight Navy):** The core typographic and structure color. Applied to headline hierarchy, structural frames, and heavy emphasis text to ensure maximum contrast against light surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Amber Craft):** Reserved for worker ratings, review score stars, caution warnings, and urgent neighborhood notice chips.
- **Functional Success (`#10B981` - Verified Mint):** Designates verified identity checks, background screenings, completed bookings, and certified insurance markers.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas is anchored by `#E2E2E3` (Soft Light Gray), providing distinct separation without eye fatigue.
  - Interactive cards, sheet surfaces, and elevated containers use `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Pure White) to create clean layer detachment from the background canvas.
  - Borders on white containers rely on `#D1D5DB` or an inner wash to retain crisp container boundaries.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve friendly authority, exceptional x-height clarity, and open apertures suited for fast mobile reading. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Tightly tracked with generous weights (`700` and `800`) to present trade names, professional titles, and verified guarantees with confidence.
- **Body:** Open, proportional text with comfortable line heights to ensure long service descriptions and cost breakdowns remain legible across outdoor brightness and small screens.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Moderately letter-spaced, high-weight instances designed for badges, category chips, distance indicators (e.g., "A 1.2 km de ti"), and ratings counters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a mobile-first fluid structure optimized for single-thumb navigation, rapid vertical scanning, and persistent bottom-reach affordances.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile screens utilize a 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` outer canvas margins and `0.75rem` gutters.
  - Tablets scale to an 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.
  - Desktop viewing constrains content to a max-width container (`1080px`) using 12 columns with generous centering.
- **Rhythm & Touch Geometry:** Every interactive target enforces a strict minimum 48x48px hit area. Structural sections adhere to 8px base grid increments, keeping component groupings predictable and easy to parse during emergency repair lookups.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering with Ambient Soft Shadows**, maintaining physical realism without visual clutter.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#E2E2E3` creates a foundational matte canvas.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` pure white surfaces placed directly on `#E2E2E3`, accompanied by an ambient diffused drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0px 4px 12px rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Sticky Bars):** Bottom action sheets, persistent booking bars, and filter menus use: `box-shadow: 0px 8px 24px rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Emergency Alerts):** Floating alerts and review dialogs sit above a `rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.5)` backdrop scrim, utilizing: `box-shadow: 0px 16px 36px rgba(0, 17, 43, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, tactile roundedness (Level 2 with extended radii for cards and interactive pills) to reinforce human warmth, community neighborhood proximity, and modern mobile app ergonomics.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded-md` / 8px):** Input fields, inline badges, and list action cells.
- **Card Corner Radius (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Professional worker profiles, booking summary cards, and map preview drawers.
- **Prominent Container Radius (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Bottom modal sheets, category selection cards, and trade filter carousels.
- **Pill Enclosures (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Status badges, distance indicators, price tags, and primary CTAs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#1764AE` with bold white text. Height: 52px on mobile. Fully rounded (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`). Subtle hover/press downscale (0.98x) and 10% darkening on active touch.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base with `#00112B` text and a 1.5px border in `#D1D5DB`. Background fills with `#FFFFFF` on hover.
- **Emergency Action:** For urgent service requests (e.g., pipe bursts, electrical outages), a high-emphasis `#00112B` background with `#F59E0B` icon accents is available.

### Trade Profile & Service Cards
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with `16px` border radii.
- Contains avatar (64x64px, rounded-2xl), verified trade badge overlay, numeric rating pill (`#F59E0B` background with 12% opacity, dark amber text, star icon), proximity tag ("A 800m"), and starting rate.
- Interactive states trigger subtle lift with 1px border shift to primary blue.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` surface, `#00112B` body text, soft border.
- Active state: `#1764AE` background, `#FFFFFF` text, zero border.
- Category chips include paired iconography for quick recognition (e.g., wrench for plomería, lightning for electricidad).

### Input Fields & Search
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`. Minimum height: 50px.
- Border: 1.5px `#D1D5DB`, transitioning smoothly to 2px `#1764AE` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(23, 100, 174, 0.15)`) on focus.
- Integrated leading icons for neighborhood location auto-complete and craft search keywords.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 24x24px to guarantee effortless thumb contact.
- Roundedness: 6px for checkboxes, 50% circular for radio options.
- Unselected: White fill with a 2px `#9CA3AF` border.
- Selected: `#1764AE` fill featuring an elevated, crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pip.

### Verification Badges & Trust Markers
- **Vecino Verificado:** Pill enclosure with `#10B981` (12% tint), bold `#065F46` label, and solid `#10B981` shield icon.
- **Rating Counter:** Amber `#F59E0B` solid star accompanied by bold score (`4.9`) and total neighborhood reviews in parentheses `(84)`.
